The Clinical Pathway

From first contact to long-term maintenance.

Six stages, each with its own clinical objective, its own client-education touchpoint, and its own measurable outcome. The pathway is identical for every client — only the contents are unique.

  1. i
    Stage 01 · First Contact

    Inquiry & intake

    Initial contact through call, form, or referral. Clinical coordinator captures presenting concern, history, prior treatments and timeline expectations. Confirmation issued within two hours during clinic hours.

    Client education at this stage What to expect at the consultation · how to prepare · which records to bring · expected duration of the first visit.
  2. ii
    Stage 02 · Diagnostic Consultation

    Examination & clinical assessment

    Unhurried clinical examination by the dermatologist. Hair density mapping, scalp dermoscopy, or skin assessment as indicated. Differential diagnosis, candidacy review, and discussion of realistic outcomes — including cases where waiting or alternatives are clinically advised.

    Client education at this stage Walkthrough of the diagnosis · explanation of underlying physiology · review of clinical options with comparative outcomes · risk and contraindication briefing.
  3. iii
    Stage 03 · Treatment Architecture

    Written plan & clinical sign-off

    A documented treatment plan: technique, session count, expected timelines, projected outcomes, contingencies, and full cost. Reviewed in person, signed only after the client has read and understood. No verbal-only undertakings.

    Client education at this stage Annotated copy of the treatment plan · pre-procedure protocol · medication and lifestyle adjustments · documented outcome expectations across 1, 6, and 12 months.
  4. iv
    Stage 04 · Procedure / Treatment

    In-clinic execution

    Procedures and sessions performed by Dr. Pratap and the senior clinical team in our purpose-built procedure rooms — never delegated to junior or unsupervised staff. Sterile protocols, real-time documentation, and intra-procedure check-ins built into every session.

    Client education at this stage Procedure-day walkthrough · pain management explanation · what each step is doing physiologically · what to watch for in the hours after.
  5. v
    Stage 05 · Recovery & Aftercare

    Structured post-procedure programme

    Day-1, week-1, month-1, month-3 reviews — built into the procedure cost, not sold as add-ons. Direct clinical line for any deviation from expected recovery. Photographic timeline maintained at every checkpoint.

    Client education at this stage Daily aftercare protocol · phase-by-phase recovery markers · what is normal & what to escalate · scheduled review milestones with photographic comparisons.
  6. vi
    Stage 06 · Long-term Maintenance

    Outcome review & ongoing care

    Six- and twelve-month outcome reviews against the original written plan. Maintenance protocols (laser top-ups, dermatology continuity, hair density preservation) documented and scheduled. Same clinician, same record, every visit.

    Client education at this stage 12-month outcome report · home-care regimen for sustained results · long-term lifestyle and dermatological factors · annual maintenance cadence.
